CROPOTTER — BRAND SYSTEM V1
Direction 04: The Mailroom Stamp
Domain: cropotter.com

IDEA
A practical label-formatting tool with the character of a well-used mailroom stamp. Condensed lettering, registration corners, indigo ink and warm paper connect the identity to the job it does.

PRIMARY MARK
Use CropOtter-stamp-indigo.png for larger, tactile brand moments. It is a transparent raster artwork, not a vector master. Its ink texture is intentional. Preserve the proportions and leave at least one eighth of the mark height clear on all sides.

CLEAN COMPANIONS
CropOtter-clean-stamp.svg is the clean, outlined vector companion. It uses Bungee lettering to carry the same stacked stamp direction into small digital uses. CropOtter-clean-stamp-reverse.svg is the warm-paper version for indigo backgrounds. CropOtter-CO-icon.svg is for browser tabs and compact brand placements. The clean companion is a redraw, not an exact tracing of the textured mark.

SIZE
Use the clean version below 180 px wide. Suggested minimum: full clean stamp 120 px; CO icon 24 px. Check the actual output on the intended screen or print surface.

COLORS
Indigo Ink    #243A79    Primary brand color, buttons and large type
Warm Paper    #F6F1E7    Primary background
Postal Orange #D85C32    Accent rules, tabs and large emphasis
Parcel Kraft  #CDB693    Supporting backgrounds and physical mockups
Carbon        #23262E    Reading text and utility details
Use indigo or carbon for reading text on paper. Use white or warm paper text on indigo. Keep orange to emphasis rather than small body text.

TYPE
Barlow Condensed Bold: headlines and campaign copy
DM Sans Regular / Bold: instructions, body copy, buttons and navigation
IBM Plex Mono Regular: sizes, formats, numbered steps and status details
Bungee Regular: clean logo companion lettering, not the general headline font
Font files and their OFL licenses are included under assets. DM Sans static instances were generated from the Google Fonts variable font; naming metadata identifies their actual styles.

VOICE
Plain, helpful and warm. Tell people what to do and what happened.
Examples: Drop your PDF here. Looking good. Your label is ready.
Primary line: Your labels. Ready to go.
Supporting line: Less fuss. More send.

GRAPHICS
Crop corners, light dividing rules, label-shaped fields and occasional postal stripes. Let warm paper provide most of the space. Use texture for brand artwork; keep controls and reading areas clean.

APPLICATIONS
The website shown is a static design concept, not a working implementation. It reflects the planned first release: upload a PDF or paste a direct PDF link, format it, then download a PDF and/or PNG. Barcode bars and address lines in the mockup are fictional illustration only.
Do not add brand decoration to exported shipping labels, barcode data or their required quiet zones. Packaging graphics shown are brand application concepts, not additional product functionality.
