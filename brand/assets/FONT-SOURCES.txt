All four families are from Google Fonts and include their SIL Open Font License files.
Barlow Condensed: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlowcondensed
DM Sans: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans
IBM Plex Mono: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ibmplexmono
Bungee: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/bungee

DM Sans static weights 400 and 700 were instantiated at optical size 14 with fontTools. Family/style names were normalized to distinguish the static instances. These copies are supplied alongside the OFL license.
